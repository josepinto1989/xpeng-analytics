XPENGAnalytics About the project

Understand your car through your own data.

XPENG Analytics started with a simple curiosity: how is the car really being used, how much energy does it consume, and what can its exported data tell us over time?

Why it exists

The XPENG app shows useful information, but I wanted a clearer and more complete view of journeys, charging, consumption, regeneration, battery behaviour and long-term trends. This project turns the files supplied through the XPENG Data Act portal into a dashboard that is easier to explore and compare.

Built to answer everyday questions.

How efficient was this journey? How much energy was recovered? What are my charging habits? Is consumption changing over time? XPENG Analytics brings those answers together in one place.

What you can do

Explore journeys

Review speed, energy, driving inputs and other available telemetry for each journey.

Understand charging

Compare AC and DC sessions, efficiency, charging curves, power and estimated losses.

Build a history

Keep adding new XPENG exports and carry the combined history in a compact .xpeng file.

Add GPS routes

Match GPX tracks to journeys automatically and explore them on a map when location data is available.

Create reports

Generate local PDF summaries for selected dates, including distance, energy, charging and optional costs.

Make your own charts

Use the available data to create personal visualisations that stay with your history.

Your data stays under your control

The analysis runs in your browser. There is no XPENG Analytics account and no application server collecting vehicle histories. You decide whether to keep a local browser copy, export a portable .xpeng file, or use the optional backup in your own Google Drive.

An independent community project

XPENG Analytics is developed and supervised by Miguel Pinto as an independent project and is not affiliated with XPENG. Feedback from other owners helps identify useful metrics, improve compatibility and decide what to build next.
