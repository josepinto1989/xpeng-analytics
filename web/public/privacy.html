XPENGAnalytics

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Your vehicle data belongs to you. XPENG Analytics processes it in your browser and does not operate an application server that receives your vehicle history.

Local processing

XPENG Data Act exports, portable .xpeng histories and GPX routes are processed on your device. By default, the data exists only in the current browser session. If you enable remembered history, one processed history is stored in that browser's private IndexedDB storage.

Vehicle histories may contain sensitive information including travel times, routes, charging behaviour and driving patterns. Portable .xpeng files are compressed, but they are not encrypted.

Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive is optional. When you explicitly connect your account and save a backup, the browser uploads one .xpeng history directly to a visible XPENG Analytics folder in your own Google Drive.

  • Access: the app requests only the drive.file permission, limited to files it creates or that you explicitly open with it.
  • Use: Drive access is used only to create, update and restore your XPENG Analytics backup.
  • Storage: Google access tokens remain in the current tab's memory. XPENG Analytics does not store your token, password or Google email address on an application server or inside the .xpeng file.
  • Sharing: XPENG Analytics does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties. Google stores the backup in your account under Google's own terms.
  • Control: disconnecting revokes the current token when possible. It does not delete your backup; you can permanently delete the file or folder directly in Google Drive.

Maps and ordinary web requests

The static host receives ordinary requests needed to load the application and may receive standard connection information such as an IP address and user agent. When a map is displayed, its tile provider receives the requests necessary to show the viewed area. Original XPENG and GPX files are not uploaded for map rendering.

Analytics and advertising

XPENG Analytics does not include behavioural analytics, advertising trackers, remote crash uploads or mandatory cloud storage. Any future change to this model will require a public review and a clear user-facing consent decision.

Deleting your data

You can reset the current session from the Data section, permanently remove remembered browser history, disconnect Google Drive and delete the Drive backup from your own account. Clearing the browser's site data also removes locally remembered application data.

Contact

XPENG Analytics is an independent community project owned by Miguel Pinto and is not affiliated with XPENG. For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub. Never attach a real vehicle export, GPX route or .xpeng history to a public issue.
